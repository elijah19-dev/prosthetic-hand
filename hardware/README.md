# Hardware records

Store verified schematics, PCB files, part references, and bills of materials
in the directories below. The PCA9685 controller IC is known; its exact
breakout board, the battery and buck modules, the ESP32 power path, servo
limits, and measured currents remain TBD. See `HARDWARE_SPECS.md`,
`docs/pinout.md`, and `docs/power_budget.md`.
